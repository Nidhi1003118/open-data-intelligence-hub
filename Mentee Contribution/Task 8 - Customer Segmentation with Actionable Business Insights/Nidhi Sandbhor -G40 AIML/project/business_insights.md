# Business Insights Report

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to identify different customer groups based on purchasing behavior and recommend suitable business strategies for each customer segment.

---

# Customer Segments

## Segment 0 – High Value Customers

Characteristics

- High income
- High spending
- Frequent purchases
- Strong customer engagement

Recommended Actions

- Loyalty rewards
- Premium membership
- Early access to new products
- Personalized recommendations

---

## Segment 1 – Regular Customers

Characteristics

- Medium spending
- Average purchase frequency
- Good engagement

Recommended Actions

- Product recommendations
- Cross-selling
- Seasonal offers
- Bundle discounts

---

## Segment 2 – At-Risk Customers

Characteristics

- Long time since last purchase
- Low engagement
- Reduced purchase frequency

Recommended Actions

- Re-engagement campaigns
- Email reminders
- Special comeback offers
- Personalized discounts

---

## Segment 3 – Low Value Customers

Characteristics

- Low spending
- Low purchase frequency
- Limited website activity

Recommended Actions

- Promotional campaigns
- Entry-level product recommendations
- Coupon offers
- Awareness campaigns

---

# Machine Learning Summary

## K-Means Clustering

Purpose

Customer Segmentation

Business Benefit

Groups customers with similar purchasing behavior.

---

## Ridge Regression

Purpose

Predict customer spending.

Business Benefit

Helps estimate customer value and future revenue.

---

## Logistic Regression

Purpose

Predict purchase response.

Business Benefit

Identifies customers likely to respond to future campaigns.

---

# Overall Business Recommendations

- Reward loyal customers with exclusive benefits.
- Re-engage inactive customers using personalized campaigns.
- Offer discounts only to price-sensitive customers.
- Recommend premium products to high-value customers.
- Increase customer retention through targeted marketing.
- Improve campaign effectiveness using predictive models.

---

# Expected Business Impact

- Better customer segmentation
- Improved marketing performance
- Higher customer retention
- Increased sales conversion
- Better customer experience
- Improved business decision making

# Conclusion

This project demonstrates how customer segmentation combined with machine learning can support data-driven business decisions. The generated customer segments help organizations understand customer behavior and improve marketing strategies through targeted recommendations.
